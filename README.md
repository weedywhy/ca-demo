# AI Indian CA App

An AI-powered Indian Chartered Accountant application with features for ITR filing, GST compliance, TDS calculation, accounting, and AI-driven advisory chat.


## Features

- **ITR Calculation** — Tax computation under new and old regimes with slab breakdowns, 87A rebate, and cess
- **GST Calculator** — CGST/SGST and IGST calculation across standard rate slabs
- **TDS Calculator** — TDS rates by payment type with applicable section references
- **Accounting** — Double-entry transaction tracking with P&L statements and balance sheet
- **Compliance Calendar** — Track due dates for GST, TDS, ROC, and ITR filings
- **AI Advisory Chat** — Conversational Indian CA expert powered by Ollama (llama3), running fully locally

---

## AI Advisory Chat 

The system prompt configures the model as an expert Indian CA with deep knowledge of:

- Income Tax Act and ITR filing
- GST compliance and returns
- TDS rules and filing
- ROC compliance
- Indian accounting standards (Ind AS / AS)
- Company law and corporate compliance
- FEMA regulations
- Payroll under Indian labour laws
